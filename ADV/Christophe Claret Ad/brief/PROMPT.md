## 1. Three concepts

### A. "The Science of Motion" ★ recommended
Built on the brand's new tagline. Most watches *show* time; Claret watches *perform* it. We show two famous "performing" complications (magnetic spheres, the daisy), then the numbers (100+ calibres, 80 world firsts), then black: "the next chapter". The logo resolves on a minute-repeater-style chime.
*Why:* it matches the real relaunch message, it's honest (no fake product), and the moving mechanisms give us strong visuals.

### B. "Loves me, loves me not"
The whole ad is a game of chance, the brand's signature (Margot daisy, Poker, 21 Blackjack). Every beat is a coin toss or a petal falling, and the brand "wins" at the end.
*Risk:* it's fun, but it doesn't fit the serious "new chapter / science" tone of the relaunch as well.

### C. "1989 → 2027"
A timeline countdown from the founding to the comeback, with each year jumping to a calibre.
*Risk:* timelines read like a corporate history video, and it would need many dated product images we may not get.

**I'm writing everything below for Concept A.** If you choose B or C, I'll rewrite it.

## 5. Music (original, made in code)

- **Idea:** a minute repeater (the chiming complication the brand is known for) turned into music. Two "hammer" tones (low and high, like hours and quarters) set the rhythm, with a soft ticking pulse at about 96 BPM.
- **Arrangement around the voice:**
  - 0–10.4 s: ticking pulse and sparse bell tones, a low pad. **At least 15 dB under the VO** whenever a word is spoken (side-chain duck from the VO).
  - 10.4–11.8 s (no VO): the music opens up. Bells cascade and a sub swell comes in.
  - 15.0 s: everything drops out except one sustained tone (« un nouveau chapitre »).
  - 16.8 s: the logo lands on a full **chime chord** (D major, resolving from D minor). It **rings out under the end card**.
- No limiter squashing the voice. The VO stays on top through level, not compression.
